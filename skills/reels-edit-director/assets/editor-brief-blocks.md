# Editor brief building blocks

Rewrite these to fit the video. They are phrasing help, never a paste-in.

**Typography**
Two fonts only: [display] for the standout phrases, [support] for everything else. Treat text as part of the frame: beside me, next to the product, or in empty space. Vary size to show what matters, keep spacing and hierarchy consistent, and stay clear of the app's buttons and caption area.

**Colour break**
At [timestamp], cut to a full-screen [colour] card with only "[line]" for about 1.5 s, then hard-cut back to camera. Only one of these.

**Music**
Use [track or mood]. Keep my voice clearly on top: music low and ducked under speech. Land [the reveal / the line] on the track's [build / drop]; do not cut everything on the beat.

**SFX**
Light sound accents only on [moments]. Under the voice, varied, no whoosh on every cut.

**Motion**
Gentle punch-ins on [lines], one animated text summary at [timestamp], one easing style throughout. No flashy transitions competing with me.

**Avoid**
Centre-bottom text for everything, more than two fonts, preset glitch or spin transitions, music louder than the voice, text under the like and comment buttons.
