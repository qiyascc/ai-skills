# Worked examples

Patterns to adapt, not templates to paste. Read one or two that match the user's format before writing a plan.

## Contrast statement · talking head · 25 s · educator

Script spine: "You don't need more views. You need more conversions."

- Concept: calm authority, typography-led.
- "MORE VIEWS" in display type, left third, soft pop.
- A beat later "MORE CONVERSIONS" enters on the right, larger, with a punch-in to 115 %.
- Support-font subtitles for the rest; only one highlighted word per line.
- Soft instrumental bed, well under the voice.
- No colour screen: typography is the signature lever.
- Restraint: 1 signature lever, 2 SFX accents, 3 punch-ins.

## Myth-buster · talking head with one reset · 30 s

- "Here's what most people get wrong…" then a hard cut to a warm cream card: "THE EDIT ISN'T THE STRATEGY" for 1.5 s, silence or one low thump.
- Back to camera for the explanation.
- Signature lever: colour screen. Everything else at intensity 1.

## Founder story · voiceover with B-roll · 45 s

- Emotional slow-build instrumental, longer held shots, music ducked under the voice.
- The track's lift lands on the money line, shown as a single display phrase.
- No SFX except natural sound from the footage.
- Signature levers: music and typography.

## Day in the life · montage · 15 to 20 s

- Recognisable upbeat track, actions cut on the beat.
- Minimal text: time stamps in the support font, top left inside the safe zone.
- One zoom-out reveal at the end.
- SFX: natural sounds only.

## Transformation · before and after · 12 s

- 0 to 3 s: the "before" with one line of display text ("6 hours of work").
- Build in the track under the process shots, cut faster as the build rises.
- The reveal lands exactly on the drop: hard cut, no transition, one restrained impact.
- Hold the result for at least 2 s so people can look at it.
- Signature levers: music and motion.

## Tutorial · 3 steps · 40 s

- Hook shows the result first, then "3 steps" as a big number.
- Each step opens with a small chapter marker (step number in display type, top left).
- Steady instrumental around 90 to 110 BPM; cuts between steps on bar lines.
- Graphic emphasis (circle or arrow) on the exact button or detail in each step.

## Music A/B exercise · for teaching

Edit the same 10 s clip twice: once with a cinematic instrumental, once with a playful beat. Compare how the personality changes with identical footage. Useful when a user insists the music "doesn't matter".
