# Mood library

Read this after you have decided the mood family. These lists are a curated starting point, not a ceiling. Trends move fast and availability differs by account type and country, so always tell the user to confirm a track in their app's audio picker before the edit is locked. You may suggest tracks beyond this list in the same spirit; label them as your own additions.

Searching tip for every track: add "instrumental", "slowed" or "sped up" in the audio picker to find voice-friendly versions.

## A. SOFT · cinematic and polished

## D. BOLD · fast and high energy

For strong hooks, opinions, fashion, fast educational content, high-retention edits.

- **High impact:** Not Like Us · Kendrick Lamar · Money So Big · Vampire Heart · Like That · 20 Min · Hell N Back
- **Unexpected, pattern interrupt:** Nissan Altima · Doechii · Param-Pam-Pam · Carlos Campos · La MuDANZA · Bad Bunny · Runaway · Dark Red

A pattern interrupt works when the picture is calm or corporate and the sound is surprising: the mismatch itself grabs attention in the first second. It fails when the picture is already loud.

## Choosing inside a family

1. **Voice present?** Strike anything whose vocal line would fight the script, unless an instrumental exists.
2. **Where is the first change?** Prefer tracks whose first build or drop lands near the money line's timestamp after trimming. `scripts/beat_grid.py` shows bar and phrase positions once you know the BPM.
3. **Recognisability:** familiar tracks help day-in-life and lifestyle posts; for educational and business content an unfamiliar instrumental keeps attention on the words.
4. **Availability:** for business accounts, anything mainstream is a risk until confirmed (see `licensing.md`).
5. **Audition:** 2 to 3 finalists on the same opening 5 to 10 s, on headphones and a phone speaker.

## Search briefs for commercial and royalty-free libraries

For business accounts, branded content, or one master posted to several platforms. Paste the words into the library search, then filter by tempo and "no vocals" when there is a voice.

| Family | Search words | Tempo filter | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOFT | cinematic piano, warm ambient, elegant strings, minimal luxury | 60 to 95 BPM | No strong melody in the first 10 s, so the voice leads |
| EMOTIONAL | nostalgic, hopeful piano build, emotional lo-fi soul, uplifting strings | 60 to 90 BPM | One clear lift within the first 20 s to land the money line on |
| UPBEAT | feel-good indie pop, sunny acoustic, happy whistle, lifestyle groove | 100 to 130 BPM | A drop or chorus you can place a reveal on, clean intro |
| BOLD | confident hip hop beat, hard 808, trap energy, punchy percussion | 120 to 150 BPM, or half-time | Something audible in the first half second |

For a "feels like" reference, name the mainstream track from this file and describe its qualities (tempo range, instruments, where it lifts), so the user can match by ear.
