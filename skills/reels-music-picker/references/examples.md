# Worked examples

Patterns to adapt. Read the one closest to the user's format.

## Founder story · voiceover · 40 s · personal brand

```
### Mood read
Founder story → warm nostalgia → EMOTIONAL

### Top picks
1. Dark Red (instrumental) · Steve Lacy: warm and slightly raw, leaves room for the voice; the chorus lift is a natural home for the money line.
2. Runaway (instrumental): slow piano build, very clear change point; heavier, suits a "we almost quit" story.
3. Golden Hour (instrumental): softer and more polished if the brand is luxury rather than personal.
Wildcard: Plage Coquillage, if the story is light and summery.

### Placement map
| Moment | Time | Track section | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00 | intro | voice on top, music low, something audible within 0.5 s |
| Money line | 0:18 | first lift | land the line on the change, duck an extra 3 dB under it |
| CTA | 0:36 | outro | fade 0.8 s or end on the last hit |

### Mix notes for the editor
Instrumental version only. Music bed about 20 dB under the voice, ducked on dense sentences. Trim the intro so the lift falls at 0:18. No beat-cutting except the reveal of the team photo.

### Licensing check
Business account: Dark Red and Runaway are major-label tracks and may be missing from the commercial library. If so, search the library for "warm piano", "nostalgic", "lo-fi soul" and pick an instrumental with a lift in the first 20 s.
```

## Transformation · before and after · 12 s · interior design

- Mood read: transformation → satisfying payoff → UPBEAT with a clear drop, or BOLD for a pattern interrupt.
- Placement: before shots on the intro, process shots cut on beats during the build, the reveal exactly on the drop, hold the result 2 s.
- Use `scripts/beat_grid.py --bpm <bpm> --offset <first downbeat> --snap <reveal time>` to move the reveal to the nearest bar.

## Talking head · tutorial · 60 s · B2B software

- Mood read: tutorial → calm competence → SOFT, steady tempo about 90 to 110 BPM.
- Pick an unfamiliar instrumental with little melody so attention stays on the words.
- Music low the whole time; no beat-sync except chapter changes on bar lines.
- Business account: stay inside the commercial library or a licensed royalty-free source.

## Strong opinion · 20 s · creator

- Mood read: hot take → confidence → BOLD.
- Start on a hit, voice enters immediately; drop the music out for half a second right before the payoff line, then bring it back on the next bar.
- If the picture is a calm desk shot, a pattern-interrupt track does the hook's job.

## Day in the life · 18 s · montage

- Mood read: routine → light joy → UPBEAT, recognisable track.
- Cut every action on the beat (here, beat-sync everywhere is the point), wide shots on builds, fast cuts on the chorus.
- Using the platform's in-app audio keeps the post linked to the audio page.
