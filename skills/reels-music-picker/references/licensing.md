# Licensing and availability

Read this for business or creator-business accounts, any branded or client content, copyright questions, or when a track might be missing. Checked in November 2026; platform terms change, so present this as guidance and tell the user to confirm in the app.

## The short version

- **Personal accounts** can usually use the platform's general music library inside the app.
- **Business accounts and branded content** often cannot. Use the platform's commercial library or a licensed royalty-free source.
- **Use the in-app audio when you can,** instead of baking music into the exported file. It stays attributed, links to the audio page, and the platform has already cleared it for that context.
- **Never** rip audio from unofficial uploads for commercial posts.

## Instagram and Facebook

- Meta's help pages say certain business accounts and certain post types do not have access to the licensed music library, so mainstream hits may be missing or muted.
- **Meta Sound Collection:** royalty-free music and sound effects (around 14,000 tracks and 1,500+ effects according to Meta's help pages) licensed for commercial or non-commercial use **inside Meta products only**. Using those tracks on TikTok or YouTube is outside the licence.
- If a track does not appear in the picker for the account, it is not available for that account. Pick a close alternative from the same mood family.

## TikTok

- **Commercial Music Library (CML):** a pre-cleared global library of about 1 million songs for business use. TikTok states businesses cannot use the general music library for commercial purposes. Business accounts see the CML in the app.
- Brands and agencies posting from personal accounts are still doing commercial use; the account type does not change what the content is.

## YouTube Shorts

- Music from the Shorts audio library: up to 90 s of a song in a Short up to 3 minutes, depending on the song. Those licences are for personal, non-commercial content, so branded Shorts can hit errors.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (in YouTube Studio): royalty-free music; YouTube states it will not receive a Content ID claim.
- **The one-minute rule:** a Short longer than one minute with an active Content ID claim is blocked worldwide until the claim is resolved. For Shorts over 60 s, use music you are certain is licensed.

## Licensed royalty-free libraries

When the platform library does not have what the user needs, or the same video goes to several platforms:

| Library | Model | Note |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Audio Library | Free | Built for YouTube; read its terms before using elsewhere |
| Epidemic Sound | Subscription | Plan decides which channels and client work are covered |
| Artlist | Subscription | Same: check the plan for client and ad use |
| Musicbed | Subscription or per licence | Cinematic, premium catalogue |
| Uppbeat | Free tier and paid | Free tier usually needs a credit in the caption |

Search these libraries with the mood words, not genre words: "warm nostalgic piano", "confident minimal beat", "euphoric travel", "tense build".

## What to tell the user, by situation

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Personal creator, track appears in picker | Use it from the in-app library, not as an exported file |
| Business account, track missing | Not licensed for this account; here are 2 to 3 alternatives in the same mood, or search the commercial library for [mood words] |
| Client or ad work | Licensed library with a plan that covers client or ad use; keep the licence certificate |
| Same master to all platforms | A licensed royalty-free track whose plan covers every platform you post to, because each platform's own library licence only covers that platform. Subscription libraries usually ask you to connect or allowlist your channels so automated claims are cleared; check the library's help pages |
| Short over 60 s | Only certainly licensed music, because of the one-minute Content ID rule |
